Inputs
states (49) and districts (432), bundled; MorphOptions defaults; NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=8. Errors are the reported errors of the final cartogram. The difference column is the largest absolute difference of any geometry bound between the two calls.

One entry point, create_flow_cartogram, replaces morph_gdf and multiresolution_morph (both deprecated). MorphOptions.grid takes an int or tuple, GridSpec(size, margin, square), LadderSpec(min_resolution, levels, margin, square), "auto" or a prebuilt Grid, and replaces grid_size (deprecated alias); a ladder runs from coarse to fine and ends at the first converged level, in create_flow_cartogram and CartogramWorkflow.morph. Voronoi premorph and the mosaic layout accept a ladder through their MorphOptions. Results of existing calls do not change.

make_tables.py regenerates data/tables.md. There are no figures: the change is an API change and the table shows that the new calls return the results of the deprecated ones.
