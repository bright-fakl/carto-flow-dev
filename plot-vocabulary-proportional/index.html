Inputs
a 3 x 3 grid of squares with two random columns and one random share; plot_partitions on the split partition, plot_dot_density with 20 dots per category.

Fourth and last part of the parameter vocabulary of the style-system proposal. facecolor binds the fill of plot_partitions (area, category, a column, a list of columns or a color; color_by is a deprecated alias), cmap accepts the category dict (palette is deprecated), the background and complement layers use *_facecolor, and plot_dot_density uses markersize and the reference_ layer. No change of output. Stacked on the Voronoi PR.

No figures: the change is parameter names. On the 3 x 3 grid the facecolors of the default, facecolor="category" with a dict, facecolor="rv" (one column) and the dot-density plot, the marker sizes and the base polygons are identical to main with the old names (np.array_equal).
