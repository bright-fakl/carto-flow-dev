One page for the convergence work behind the tracking issue 109, now closed. The stall rule (including its scaling with the grid and its attention to the lagging regions), best iterate, recompute-on-rise, exact rasterization, error floor, adaptive step, presets, the resolution check of the multiresolution ladder, pre-flight validation and the validity options that keep the output polygons valid are merged; counties work with the ladder and the hierarchical morph (129) was closed as not planned. Open items are the benchmarks of the other cartograms (113) and the rubber-sheet alternative (79); the GSM solver (75) is on hold. Links to the evidence pages.

This page replaces reading the exploration pages in order. Issue 109 is closed; each open item has its own issue. Updated 2026-10-27 with the work merged since 2026-10-17. Each statement links to the page that holds the figures and tables. It contains no new measurements.

Merged

  • Stall rule, best iterate, recompute on rise (#110, pr110-stall-detection). Progress is judged per window of at least 10 iterations and only in a component that is still violated; a run that does not converge returns its best iterate; the velocity field is also recomputed early when the score rises (recompute_on_rise, default 0.01), with recompute_every as the maximum interval. Origin: adaptive-recompute-experiment.

topicwhat was decided and mergedevidencePR
Area-weighted density (#95)"exact" coverage is the default rasterization. It costs no more than cell-centre sampling, conserves mass, and removes the sub-cell failures: the coarse multiresolution plateau, the grid-128 anisotropy failures and the DC error (4.4 % to 0.8 % at grid 256). It does not change the sawtooth, the counties plateau or the border drift.exploration-area-coverage, exact-density-coverage#119
Error floor (#87)A grid-independent floor of 1e-3 times the mean target area, instead of one grid cell, so the reported error is comparable across grids and does not hide small regions on coarse grids.error-floor#121
Step control (#94)The late phase is a limit cycle: the fastest point moves dt cells whatever the error, so the areas alternate around their targets with an amplitude proportional to dt. A step that shrinks when more than 30 % of the regions change the sign of their error (never growing back), plus a cap on the first steps of a restart, removes it: 32 of 32 tight-tolerance runs converge against 17, restarts from a converged result take 1 iteration instead of 4.adaptive-step-control, exploration-adaptive-step#123 (adaptive_dt, default on)
Exact rasterizer fixA ring with a small self-intersecting loop was judged counter-clockwise, so its region dropped out of the density field (a stalled districts run converges).exact-ring-orientation#124
Output validity (#85)The morph warns about self-intersecting regions of the result; repair=True makes them valid. The crossings are not prevented.output-validity#125
Presets (#115)The iteration limits of the presets are raised so that they converge on states and districts; preset_balanced equals the defaults. The larger default dt was not adopted: it saves 20 to 40 % at default tolerances but costs 1.5 to 2.7 times more at tight tolerances, adds invalid polygons on districts above 0.4 and grows the border drift.preset-review, exploration-default-dt#127
Multiresolution (#93)A warning when the smallest target region covers fewer than 2.5 cells at the coarsest grid, "auto" for the ladder, one entry point (create_flow_cartogram), n_iter as the total of a ladder. Refinement on stagnation was measured and not adopted.pr128-multires-auto-resolution, exploration-multires-start, exploration-multires-stall#128, #130, #131, #132
Border drift of carried points (#86)The drift is inherent to the flow (it depends on dt times recompute_every, not on the rasterizer). Cartogram.get_landmarks(iteration, uniform=True) remaps the distance of point landmarks to the border of their region so that landmarks that were uniform are uniform again (border-band z-score 0.47 against 4.28 for the carried dots, 0.63 on the original map).pr133-landmark-uniform, exploration-border-drift#133
Pre-flight validation (#78)Invalid input geometries, duplicate vertices and coverage overlaps are reported with clear errors before the morph starts.#160
Stall rule and the grid (#166)A window has to improve by stall_min_efficiency (0.05) times the distance the boundaries moved divided by the area-weighted region size, instead of a fixed 2 %, so runs on fine grids that are converging are no longer ended. Arizona's counties converge at grids 512 and 1024; runs on the default grid are unchanged.stall-rule-grid-scaling, exploration-county-hierarchical-prototype#171
Square grid cell sizeA square grid has a cell size equal to its coordinate spacing, so a vertex is no longer placed in the wrong cell when interpolating the displacement. It affects LadderSpec and grid_square=True.#170
Counties (#129, #167)A ladder from grid 256 to 2048 with the default options gives a county cartogram; the hierarchical morph is not needed, and #129 was closed as not planned on 2026-10-26. load_us_census(level="county") provides the data (#169), the loaders take max_segment_length (#175), and a warning names a recommended segment length when more than 0.5 % of the segments are expected to outgrow 4 cells of the finest grid. A gallery example shows the result.exploration-county-hierarchical-prototype, segment-density-warning, county-gallery-example, census-county-level, census-max-segment-length#172, #173, #169, #175
Valid output polygons (#178, #179)validity="repair-crossings" tests for crossings and repairs them during the morph; validity="repair-quadtree" moves the vertices with the piecewise-affine map of a graded quadtree mesh and suits input whose segments are much longer than the grid cells. On the 3,108 counties (1 km input) repair-crossings gives 0 invalid polygons instead of 57. Both are opt-in.exploration-validity-research, validated-windows-pr, quadtree-windows-pr#180, #181
Stall rule and the lagging regions (#177)A third component of the stall rule judges the lagging error (the total log2 error above the tolerances), and the best iterate is chosen by the same measure; only the finest level of a ladder follows it (wait_for_lagging_regions, default on). On the county ladder (repair-crossings, budget 3000) the finest level converges after 1842 iterations instead of stalling after 200.exploration-stall-lagging-regions, exploration-stall-lagging-component, exploration-stall-after-validity, stall-lagging-pr#186
Unresolved small regions (#177)MorphOptions.area_tol_cells (default off) stops a morph early when small regions are within k cells of their target; Cartogram.n_unresolved counts the excused regions; a stalled morph warns and names the continuation workflow.morph(stall_patience=None, n_iter=N). It is an early-stopping option, not an accuracy option.exploration-unresolved-regions, unresolved-regions-pr#185

Open

  • Benchmarks and performance pages for the other cartograms (#113, open). The county prototype lists counties as a candidate for the benchmarks (exploration-county-hierarchical-prototype).
  • Dougenik rubber-sheet method (#79, open, low priority): or a docs note on why it is not offered.
  • County tail: on the county ladder with repair-crossings and a budget of 1000 iterations the finest level ends with a mean error of 0.46 % and 16 counties above 10 %; a budget of 3000 converges after 1842 iterations (stall-lagging-pr).
  • Follow-ups of the adaptive step, only if needed: a shorter restart window, the slow convergence of sub-cell regions such as DC, the untested "center" rasterizer with prescale_components; the districts multiresolution at 0.5 % / 1 % and dt 0.4 stalls in both modes.

On hold

  • GSM (Gastner, Seguy, More 2018) solver and step scale (#75, open): exploration-overview. The spatial pattern does not help; the physical step magnitude cuts the cost 3 to 5 times on states but adds invalid polygons and fails on districts and counties; the full integrator costs 1.8 to 2.2 times the baseline in this implementation. The benchmark against the R packages is also on hold.

Cost model used on these pages

Cost is in plain-step units: iterations plus field recomputes times the ratio of one recompute to one step. The ratio is 9, 12, 24 and 71 on the states at grids 128, 256, 512 and 1024. Wall times from earlier drafts were measured under load and are not used.
