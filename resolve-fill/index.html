Inputs
the 49 US states (flow plots, 64 x 64 grid), a 3 x 3 grid (Voronoi plots).

The static flow plots and the Voronoi plots each had their own copy of the rules for facecolor (None, a string that is a column, a built-in quantity or a color, a numeric array, a color array). They share resolve_fill in _plot_params.py, which returns (kind, value). The flow animations, the proportional plot_partitions and the symbol plot keep their own handling on top of classify_string. No change of output.

No figures: a refactor. The facecolors of the default, column, built-in and array fills of the flow plots and of the categorical, column, area_error_pct and comparison plots of the Voronoi module are identical to the output before the change (np.array_equal).
