Inputs
49 states (bundled) with poverty counts, dot density with n_dots = round(max / 25000), normalization "maximum" (about 1,480 dots), 8 seeds; MorphOptions defaults with area_scale 1e-6 (grid 256); NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=8. The border-band z-score counts the dots inside a band of 6 % of sqrt(area) along the border of their own region against the number expected for uniform dots (binomial, summed over regions).

The morph sums the divergence of the velocity field along the path of every landmark vertex (Cartogram.landmark_area_scaling) at no measurable cost, and Cartogram.get_landmarks(uniform=True) uses it to remap the distance of point landmarks to the border of their region so that landmarks that were uniform before the morph are uniform again. On the states with 8 seeds of about 1,480 dots the mean border-band z-score is 4.28 for the carried dots, 0.86 with uniform=True and 0.63 on the original map; the median dot moves 0.7 % of the width of its region.

make_figures.py regenerates the figures and data/tables.md; it uses get_landmarks(uniform=True) of the library. The cost: within the same checkout, disabling the accumulation does not change the morph time (0.229 against 0.228 s at grid 256 and 0.53 against 0.54 s at grid 512, 1,480 dots, minimum of 5 runs); get_landmarks(uniform=True) takes about 0.2 s for these dots. More on the mechanism, the measurements and the alternatives that were tried is on exploration-border-drift.
