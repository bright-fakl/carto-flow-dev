Inputs
ACS 5-year 2020 via censusdis (scripts/build_census_snapshot.py, new county level); ESRI:102008, coverage-simplified at 1000 m, densified to 5 km segments.

load_us_census(level="county") returns 3,108 contiguous counties from a bundled ACS 2020 snapshot (2.9 MB), with population, race and poverty columns. Black and Asian counts are zero in 95 and 258 counties, which makes them a second, harder value distribution for county flow cartograms.

Figures

Dataset

  • 3,221 rows in the bundled file (all states, DC and Puerto Rico); contiguous_only=True (default) drops Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico and keeps DC, giving 3,108.
  • Columns as for the state level plus County Name, COUNTY (the county FIPS part) and State Name; Region and Division as before.
  • Geometries are valid as loaded and after simplify=2000 (the data validity tests cover all three loads).
