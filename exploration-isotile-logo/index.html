Inputs
Tile geometry from isotile (branch phase-1-package: IsohedralTiling types 1, 20, 28, 29 and 34, fill_region, TileSet.vertices, centroids, polygons, colors, aspect, cell, adjacency, translation_u/v, edge_transforms). Palette of carto-flow (blue #2c6e9e / #85b7e0, amber #c27400 / #facc38). Marks generated by marks.py (96 x 96 viewBox, no fonts except the live-text lockup draft); figures by fig.py (cairosvg, nearest-neighbor 2x enlargement of 1:1 renders).

Five mark concepts for isotile (a pentagon tiling patch, interlocking curved tiles, a lattice cell, an it monogram of hexagon tiles, one tile with its neighbor links), drawn in the carto-flow palette from real isotile geometry and shown at 128, 64, 32 and 16 px on the light and dark surfaces and in one color. At 16 px only the simplified favicon variants of the neighbor-links and lattice-cell marks keep a clear amber shape; the tile patches become a cluster of specks with one amber speck, and the monogram does not hold. The recommendation is the pentagon patch (a_patch, with the four-tile favicon variant a_patch_fav), ahead of the interlocking tiles; its weak point is that it is generic at 16 px.

marks.py <out_dir> writes <concept>-light.svg and <concept>-dark.svg for every concept and favicon variant, and lockup-<concept>-<variant>.svg for the two lockup drafts (committed in svg/). fig.py draws the figures. Tile outlines are isotile prototiles placed by fill_region; each tile is inset by a gap (shapely buffer) so the marks have real gaps and work on any surface. The tint of a blue tile is a fixed opacity level chosen from the tile's color index in the tiling.

Observations

  • Nothing from the carto-flow blob is repeated: no four-region cross, no wavy gaps. The shared elements are the two palettes and one amber element among blue ones (the role of the amber regions).
  • The tile patches (a_patch, b_escher) read as tiles from 64 px up. At 32 px the gaps are one pixel or less and the tints carry the separation; at 16 px they are clusters. The favicon variants (fewer tiles, gaps 3.6 to 4.4 units of 96) are better at 16 px but still specks.
  • b_escher is the closest to what people picture for tilings. Weak points: the curves come from a seeded random generator and the outlines read as leaves (maple or ivy), not as creatures; a hand-drawn tile could fix that. The curves are not from the library (isotile has no edge curves yet), so the mark would need a check once it has them.
  • c_cell is the most direct statement of the library's vocabulary (cell, aspects, translation vectors), keeps a clear amber diamond at 32 px and has a working favicon variant. Its parallelogram is tall and narrow, so the mark does not fill a square, and it reads as a gem or diamond more than as a tiling.
  • e_links is the most legible at small sizes in the favicon variant, but it reads as a network or molecule icon; the tiling is implied only by the faint neighbor polygons of the full version.
  • d_letter fails: a letter made of hexagon tiles is a narrow column (the i has an aspect ratio of about 1:4.5), the t of hexagons looks like a cross, and neither survives 32 px.

The lighter blue tints (opacity 0.34 to 0.74) are decorative and have low contrast to the surface; no mark relies on them for its outline.

Recommendation: a_patch, with a_patch_fav as the favicon. It is built from an actual isotile tiling, has the structure of the carto-flow mark (blue pieces, one amber piece) and its favicon variant is still a recognizable tile cluster. Weak points: it is the most generic of the strong candidates (a pentagon cluster also reads as cells, a honeycomb or a panel pattern); at 16 px the tiles are 3 to 4 pixels and the amber tile is a speck, so the favicon works through the amber dot and the pinwheel silhouette, not through detail; the pentagon is a Cairo-like shape found by a numeric search; the blue tints rely on opacity and are dim on the dark surface. If a more characteristic mark matters more than favicon strength, b_escher is the alternative, at the cost of the leaf resemblance and a hand-tuned tile; if 16 px legibility matters most, c_cell_fav or e_links_fav are safer.

Not done

  • No final assets: no outlined wordmark (the lockups use live DejaVu Sans Bold, which depends on the font being installed; the final one would be outlined from a font whose license allows it, as in carto-flow), no favicon set, no social preview, no usage note.
  • The monogram was not developed beyond the hexagon version; a letter drawn from tiles of another type was not tried.
  • The curved tile of b_escher is not a designed shape; no creature-like tile was drawn, and the library has no edge curves to validate against.
  • No check against existing logos beyond avoiding the obvious ones; leaf-like and hexagon or pentagon clusters are common motifs.
  • Other types of the pentagon and rhombille families (27, 29, 33, 37) were only screened visually.
