Inputs
US states (49 incl. DC), congressional districts (432), bundled; 3,108 counties (prepared set, ESRI:102008, total_votes, area_scale 1e-6, not bundled). MorphOptions at the preset values or as stated, with the stall rule, refresh-on-rise, adaptive_dt and exact rasterization at their defaults; NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=8. True error is the output area against the population share of the total output area. Cost = iterations + field recomputes x cost of one recompute in plain steps (states 9/12/24/71 at grids 128/256/512/1024, districts 18/21/23/47, counties 21/47.5/28.5/60).

With the current algorithm the three presets stopped before converging (30, 100 and 300 iterations against 43, 112 and 425 needed on the states): the bundled states ended with a mean area error of 42 %, 13 % and 49 %. The iteration limits are raised (150, 500, 1000) and the presets documented; dt 0.3 for balanced is not adopted because the districts end with more self-intersecting regions; premorph needs no preset of its own; none of the presets converges on the counties.

The scripts are in this directory: presets_now.py (LABEL=before on main, LABEL=after on the branch), preset_matrix.py, hq_dt.py and make_tables.py (writes data/tables.md, the tables below); common.py holds the datasets, the cost model and one measured run. The counties run when COUNTIES_PARQUET points at the prepared set.

Findings

  • The three presets stopped before converging. Their n_iter (30, 100, 300) dates from before the stall rule, the exact rasterization and the adaptive step; the current algorithm needs 43, 112 and 425 iterations on the states and 79, 145 and 519 on the districts at the preset values. The presets returned a best iterate that had not converged (states: true mean error 42 %, 13 %, 49 %) without a warning.
  • Fix: n_iter 150, 500, 1000. Grid, dt and tolerances are unchanged. All three converge on the states and the districts within their tolerances.
  • preset_balanced() equals MorphOptions(). It stays as a named alias (the docs, the examples and premorph use it).
  • dt: 0.2 stays. With the adaptive step a larger dt cuts the cost by 25 to 35 %, but the districts end with more self-intersecting regions (12 at dt 0.2, 19 at 0.3 and 0.4 at grid 256, 20 to 38 at grid 128), and at grid 512 they stall at dt 0.4 with the default tolerances and at dt 0.3 with the tight ones (matrix below). The earlier suggestion of dt 0.3 for balanced (exploration-default-dt) predates the adaptive step and is not adopted.
  • high_quality keeps dt 0.1. Against dt 0.2 it needs about twice the iterations (425 against 216 on the states, 519 against 268 on the districts) and ends with half the maximum error and fewer self-intersecting districts (11 against 20). At tolerances of 0.5 % / 1 % dt 0.2 stalls on the districts.
  • fast is a preview. On the states it is within 11 % maximum error; on the districts at grid 128 the maximum error is 22 % and 32 of 432 regions end self-intersecting.
  • premorph builds on preset_balanced().copy_with(n_iter=400); the flow morph converges in 112 (states) and 145 (districts) iterations, so 400 leaves room and no preset of its own is needed.
  • Counties. None of the presets converges (stalls at 120, 210 and 50 iterations): most targets are below one grid cell (exploration-counties-subcell). high_quality single-level at grid 512 stalls after 50 iterations because it starts too far from the target.
