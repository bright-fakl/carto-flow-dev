Inputs
Python 3.10, numpy, shapely (tests); best of 15 runs for the timing

fill_region takes offset=(u, v), the lattice phase in multiples of the two translation vectors of the placed tiling, and selects the tiles in one cover pass. Needed by Tiling.with_offset of adoption step 3. Nothing to compare visually; the page gives the written evidence.

Issue 7 of isotile. IsohedralTiling.fill_region(..., offset=(u, v)) anchors the lattice at the region center plus u * translation_u + v * translation_v of the placed tiling. The default (0.0, 0.0) gives the previous output. Non-finite values and a wrong shape raise ValueError.
