The figures are not committed: `docs/diagrams/make_flow_algorithm_figures.py` draws them and the mkdocs hook `docs/hooks/generate_diagrams.py` runs it before every build, into `docs/generated/diagrams` (gitignored). The page shows the diffusion idea as SVG; the figure here is a PNG of the same drawing.

## Figures

figure: fig_diffusion_idea.png — Intuition: a dense region grows and a sparse region shrinks until both have the same density.
figure: fig_electrostatic_analogy.png — The solve read as electrostatics: the density minus the mean is a charge, the velocity is the electric field, so points move away from the dense (positive) region and toward the sparse (negative) one.
figure: fig_1d_mechanics.png — The loop on three regions of a line at iteration 0, on the way (280) and at convergence (2244): the regions, their density against the target density, and the velocity.
