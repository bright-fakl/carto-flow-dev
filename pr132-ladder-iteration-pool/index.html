Inputs
the cases of exploration-multires-stall (states with area-like and lognormal sigma-2 values, districts with smooth sigma-2, inverse-area and population values, 3,108 counties not bundled); tolerances 1 % / 3 %, n_iter 500, adaptive_dt on, NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=8. True error is the output area against the target share of the total output area.

A ladder (LadderSpec or "auto" in MorphOptions.grid) shares one iteration total: each level may use what the previous levels left, and the ladder stops when the total is used up. A list of options keeps one n_iter per level. Ladders that end before the total (states, districts) are unchanged; counties at 500 iterations stop at grid 1024 and the districts with smooth sigma-2 values end completed.

make_tables.py regenerates data/tables.md. "Per level" is the ladder with a list of options (500 iterations for every level, the old behavior); "total" is a single MorphOptions with n_iter=500. There are no figures.
