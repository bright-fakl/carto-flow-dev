Inputs
bundled US states; a population flow cartogram (grid 64, 30 iterations); a population Voronoi cartogram (RasterBackend 256, 100 iterations).

VelocityPlotOptions(norm=) and the Voronoi plot_displacement(norm=) take a scale (linear, sqrt, log, auto or a Normalize) for the magnitude that sets arrow color, opacity and the colorbar; sqrt joins the color scales. The density and velocity plots and the Voronoi displacement arrows take the palettes of the style, and the flow convergence plots the series colors.

make_figures.py draws the plots on the PR branch. The colors of the velocity (color by magnitude, direction, opacity by magnitude, solid), density (none, ratio, difference), convergence and Voronoi displacement plots under style="classic" are identical to main.

Scales

Palettes of the style

What changes

scalesVelocityPlotOptions.norm, plot_displacement(norm=) of the Voronoi cartogram (function and method); "sqrt" is a color scale everywhere (PowerNorm(0.5))
palettesdensity cmap (sequential, diverging for ratio and difference), velocity magnitude cmap, Voronoi displacement cmap: the style, then the former default
series colorsplot_convergence and the workflow convergence plot: series_color, highlight_color
colorbarsthe density and velocity colorbars (static plots and animations) are drawn by the style; legend_kwargs["label"] replaces the label
cyclic palettePalettes(cyclic=...) is a fourth palette role, used for the direction of the velocity: hsv in default and dark (the ends of twilight are as light or as dark as the surface, so arrows in those directions vanish), twilight in classic
previewspreview_modulator of the density and anisotropy modules take style=: diverging palette for the density, sequential for the arrows, cyclic for angles, diverging for speed changes, colorbars of the style
tilingsTilingResult.plot, plot_tile, plot_prototile take style=: stroke color for outlines, cyclic palette for color_by="aspect", sequential for the other modes, categorical palette for the edge shapes
not yetnothing known: a scan of the public plot and animate functions found none without style= (save_animation takes the style from the figure)
