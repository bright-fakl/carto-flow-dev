Inputs
bundled US states, MorphOptions(grid=64, n_iter=20); the flow gallery examples plot_flow_animation, plot_flow_animation_density, plot_flow_animation_velocity.

First part of the parameter vocabulary of the style-system proposal. facecolor is the fill (a column, a built-in quantity, an array or a color) of the flow plots and animations, replacing column, color_by and color_values; colorbar, colorbar_label and colorbar_kwargs become legend, legend_label and legend_kwargs. Old names stay as deprecated aliases. Strings resolve as column, then built-in quantity, then color, with a warning on ambiguity. No change of output. The symbol, Voronoi and proportional modules follow in separate PRs.

No figures: the change is parameter names and the resolution of string arguments; the output is unchanged. On the bundled states the facecolors of the default plot, facecolor="Population" and facecolor="density" are identical to main with column= (np.array_equal on the RGBA arrays of the collection).
